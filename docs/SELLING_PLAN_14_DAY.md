# RetainAI: 14-day selling plan for Calendly service businesses

Draft for root and owner review (2026-11-12). Nothing here has been sent,
posted or paid for. Every claim below must match the live product on the day
it is used; the "Claims we can make" list is the source of truth.

## Who to sell to first

Owner-operated Canadian service businesses that already book through Calendly
and rely on repeat visits: hair and barber studios, estheticians, massage and
physio clinics, tutors and coaches, personal trainers, pet groomers, and
home-service trades with recurring maintenance.

Why: they already have a customer list and a booking history, so RetainAI can
show value in the first session (who is overdue, who to follow up with). Their
pain is no-shows, customers who quietly stop rebooking, and follow-ups that
live in the owner's head.

## Claims we can make (verify before each use)

- C$20/month for the full workspace, with a 14-day trial (`/pricing`).
- Import contacts from CSV or vCard, with presets for common CRM exports
  (Settings > Imports).
- AI-drafted follow-ups that the owner reviews before anything is sent
  (AI Studio: "AI never sends until you choose to").
- Rebooking suggestions from completed-visit intervals, and a Value Receipt
  that traces revenue to real recorded payments.
- Calendly: read-only booking import. **Only say "connects to Calendly"
  once production Calendly approval is complete**; until then say "import
  your Calendly contacts by CSV export".
- Canadian, owner-run, with a 10-minute setup walkthrough
  (`publicContent.bookingUrl`).

Do not claim: customer counts, testimonials, revenue uplift percentages,
"automatic" sending, integrations not listed above, or awards and audits.

## The 14 days

| Day | Goal | Actions (owner, about 1-2 hours a day) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target list | Build a sheet of 60 local businesses whose website or Instagram links to a Calendly page. Note the niche, city, booking link and one public detail per business. |
| 2 | Proof assets | Record a 90-second screen video on a demo workspace with sample data only: import CSV → overdue customers → AI follow-up draft → owner edits and sends. Take 3 screenshots for DMs. |
| 3 | Warm network | Message 15 people you know who run or work at service businesses. Ask for a 10-minute look, not a sale. |
| 4-5 | First cold wave | Send 20 personal emails or DMs a day (see template A). Each one names their Calendly page and one specific detail. |
| 6 | Walkthroughs | Run 10-minute walkthroughs from the booking link. Set up their import live and leave them with one drafted follow-up. |
| 7 | Review | Count replies, walkthroughs and trials started. Keep the niche with the best reply rate; drop the weakest one. |
| 8-9 | Second wave | 20 messages a day to the best niche. Follow up once (template B) with anyone from days 4-5 who has not replied. |
| 10 | Partners | Approach 5 bookkeepers, web designers or Calendly consultants who serve these niches about the partner program (template C). |
| 11 | Trial check-ins | Message every trial user personally: "Did your import work? Want me to set up your first rebooking list?" |
| 12 | Content | Publish one practical post or article: "How to find customers who stopped rebooking (with or without software)". It should be useful without RetainAI. |
| 13 | Conversion | For trials ending soon, offer a second walkthrough. Never pressure. Cancellation stays one click away. |
| 14 | Retrospective | Record what worked: niche, message, reply rate, trial→paid. Write the next 14 days based only on that data. |

Daily targets to measure (honest numbers, kept in a private sheet): messages
sent, replies, walkthroughs booked, trials started, trials converted.

## Templates (personalize every one; never mass-send)

**A, first message**
> Hi {name}, I saw {business} books through Calendly ({detail}). I run
> RetainAI, a small Canadian CRM that shows which customers are due to rebook
> and drafts a follow-up you approve before it sends. Would a 10-minute look
> with your own customer list be useful? No pressure either way.
> Mateo, owner@retainai.ca

**B, single follow-up**
> Hi {name}, just following up once. If it helps, here is a 90-second video of
> the rebooking list: {link}. Happy to leave it there if it's not a fit.

**C, partners**
> RetainAI pays partners 30% of paid base membership revenue for a referred
> customer's first 12 months, with a 30-day hold and clear refund rules
> (retainai.ca/partners). If your clients book through Calendly, I'd like to
> show you how it works for them.

## Rules

- Canada's anti-spam law (CASL): only send commercial messages to people who
  have given consent, or to published business addresses where the message
  is relevant to their business role. Always identify yourself, include a way
  to opt out, and honour opt-outs immediately.
- One follow-up maximum per contact.
- No purchased lists, no automated mass email, no paid ads in this phase.
- Demo only with sample data; never show a real customer's records.
