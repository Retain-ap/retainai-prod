# RetainAI: 14-day selling plan for Calendly service businesses

Draft for root and owner review (2026-11-12). Nothing here has been sent,
posted or paid for. Every claim must match the live product on the day it is
used; the "Claims we can make" list is the source of truth.

## Approach for these 14 days

**Free public posts and inbound leads only.** No cold email, no cold DMs, no
purchased or scraped lists, no paid ads, and no scheduled outreach of any
kind. People find RetainAI through useful public posts and the website; they
reach out or sign up themselves, and every later message goes only to people
who asked for it.

### Gate before any commercial outreach (not met today)

Direct commercial messages (email, DM, text) stay off until **all** of these
are verified and recorded by the owner:

1. A lawful consent basis for each recipient under Canada's anti-spam law
   (CASL): express consent, or an implied-consent basis that has been checked
   for that specific person. A business address being published somewhere
   does **not** by itself supply consent.
2. A truthful sender mailing address approved by the owner, shown in every
   commercial message. None is approved yet.
3. A working unsubscribe that is honoured promptly.
4. An approved sender identity (who the message is from, and from which
   account).

Until then, replies to people who contact RetainAI first are fine, because
they are answering that person's own request.

## Who the posts are for

Owner-operated Canadian service businesses that book through Calendly and
depend on repeat visits: hair and barber studios, estheticians, massage and
physio clinics, tutors and coaches, personal trainers, pet groomers, and
home-service trades with recurring maintenance. Their pain: no-shows,
customers who quietly stop rebooking, follow-ups kept in the owner's head.

## Claims we can make (verify before each use)

- C$20/month for the full workspace, with a 14-day trial (`/pricing`).
- Import contacts from a CSV or vCard file, with presets for common CRM
  exports (Settings > Imports).
- AI-drafted follow-ups that the owner reviews before anything is sent
  (AI Studio: "AI never sends until you choose to").
- Rebooking suggestions from completed-visit intervals, and a Value Receipt
  that traces revenue to real recorded payments.
- Calendly: a read-only booking import. **Say "connects to Calendly" only
  after production Calendly approval is complete.** Do not tell anyone to
  export a CSV from Calendly unless that export has been verified for their
  Calendly plan; say instead "import a CSV or vCard of the contacts you
  already have".
- Canadian and owner-run, with a 10-minute setup walkthrough that people can
  book themselves (`publicContent.bookingUrl`).

Do not claim: customer counts, testimonials, revenue-uplift percentages,
"automatic" sending, integrations not listed above, or awards and audits.

## Where posts may go

Only places whose rules allow it, posted from an identity the owner has
approved:

- RetainAI's own website. retainai.ca has no blog yet, so each guide is a new
  public page that goes through a reviewed code change before it is published.
- App-owned social and publishing accounts, **once they are supplied**. They
  do not exist yet, so drafts wait.
- Community spaces (local business groups, niche forums, subreddits) **only
  where self-promotion is explicitly permitted**. Read and follow each
  space's rules; prefer useful answers over announcements.

## The 14 days

| Day | Goal | Actions (owner, about 1 hour a day) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Groundwork | List 10 community spaces where these businesses gather, and record each one's self-promotion rules. Keep only those that allow it. |
| 2 | Proof assets | Record a 90-second screen video on a demo workspace with sample data only: import a file → overdue customers → AI follow-up draft → owner edits and sends. Take 3 screenshots. |
| 3 | Website guide | Draft, then publish through a reviewed page change, "How to find customers who stopped rebooking (with or without software)" on retainai.ca. It should be useful on its own and link to the trial and the walkthrough booking page. |
| 4-5 | Helpful answers | In the permitted spaces, answer real questions about no-shows, rebooking and follow-ups. Mention RetainAI only where the rules allow it and it actually helps. |
| 6 | Walkthroughs | Run the 10-minute walkthroughs people booked themselves. Set up their import live, using only their own data and with their permission. |
| 7 | Review | Count inbound walkthroughs, trials started and which post brought each. Keep what worked. |
| 8-9 | Second guide | Draft a niche guide for the best-performing niche (for example "A rebooking routine for barbers") and publish it the same way. |
| 10 | Partners (inbound) | Publish a post explaining the partner program for bookkeepers, web designers and Calendly consultants, linking to retainai.ca/partners. Respond only to those who apply or ask. |
| 11 | Trial check-ins | In-app or reply messages to trial users who signed up, about their setup: "Did your import work? Want help setting up your first rebooking list?" Service messages only, no promotion. |
| 12 | Social drafts | Prepare 5 posts for the app-owned accounts. Publish only once those accounts are supplied and approved. |
| 13 | Conversion help | Offer trial users whose trial ends soon an optional second walkthrough on the same thread. No pressure; cancellation stays one click away. |
| 14 | Retrospective | Record the results: which posts and spaces brought walkthroughs, trials and paid conversions. Plan the next 14 days from that data only. |

Measures (private sheet, honest numbers): posts published, inbound
walkthrough bookings, trials started, trials converted, source of each.

## Rules

- No commercial email, DM or text to anyone who has not contacted RetainAI or
  given express consent, until the outreach gate above is met.
- No purchased lists, scraping, automated messaging or paid ads.
- Demo only with sample data; never show a real customer's records.
- The partner offer is quoted exactly as above, never rounded up or extended.
